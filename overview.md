MusicBox  Features Overview

- **Collection management:** Allows users to save and organize their vinyl records, cassettes, and CDs.
- **Music Search:** Helps users search for what they already collect and what they want to listen to whenever time.
- **Digital Version Booklets:** Users can save their physical booklets copy as a digital version; it saves time to find booklets and reduce potential to cause damage.
- **Liner Notes:** provides access to album information and liner notes.
- **Connect to Music App:** Users can listen to music on their device instead of their physical collection when they want to protect it and extend its life.
