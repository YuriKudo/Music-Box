# MVP Scope & Feature Backlog

## The Ultimate Product Vision

MusicBox is a collection management app designed for users who own vinyl records, cassette tapes, and CDs. It allows users to access digital versions of booklets containing lyrics and liner notes.

## The Launch Goal (v1.0 MVP)

To launch a functional version by **April 1, 2027**, focusing strictly on the core value of the application.

## Feature Prioritization Matrix

### Must Have (MVP Lineup)

- **Collection Management:** Allows users to save and organize their vinyl records, cassette tapes, and CDs.

- **Music Search:** Helps users search for albums and artists in their collections, making it easier to find specific music whenever they want.

- **Digital Booklet:** Allows users to access digital copies of their physical booklets. This saves time when searching for booklets and reduces the risk of damaging the original copies.

### Nice to Have (Post-Launch Backlog)

- **Liner Notes:** Provides additional album information, including recording details, artist information, and background stories.

- **Connect to Music Apps:** Allows users to listen to music through digital music services instead of playing their physical media, helping protect their collections and extend their lifespan.
