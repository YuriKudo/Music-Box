# Risk Mitigation & Technical Constraints
## Core Technical Risks
*   **Highest Risk Feature:** Digital booklet
*   **The Hazard:** Digital version booklet may need significant data storage, especially when booklets contain high-resolution images.

## The Pivot Strategy
If our primary implementation for this feature fails or takes too long, our backup plan is to:
* Alternative approach 1: Use external link to show booklet as a pdf form their local storage
* Alternative approach 2: Use sample booklet image to show how the digital booklets works, even if users cannot upload their physical booklet yet.
